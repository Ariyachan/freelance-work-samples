# Why Terence Crawford Can't Leave Boxing Alone

## Intro

Terence Crawford retired from fighting, then found another role inside boxing.

He had walked away unbeaten. The decision, he said, had already been made before he faced Canelo Alvarez. Yet retirement still left him discussing the fighters, rivalries and arguments around the sport.

So what does walking away mean when boxing keeps giving you a reason to speak?

## Chapter 1 — A different role

In December 2025, Crawford announced his retirement with 42 wins and no defeats. His victory over Canelo Alvarez had made him the undisputed super-middleweight champion and a world champion in a fifth weight division.

At The Ring's awards, he explained the cost behind that decision. He would miss the competition, but protecting his health mattered.

By a July 2026 interview, he was describing a different challenge: working as a boxing analyst. Crawford admitted that this unfamiliar role had made him nervous, and said he wanted to improve.

The pressure had changed. His connection to boxing was still there—and conversations about his former rivals were giving it another outlet.
