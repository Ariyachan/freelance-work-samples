03 / PAID DESIGN REVIEW

Game-discovery usability review

A review of five public discovery flows, with six prioritized findings, supporting screenshots and five illustrated report pages.

Read the selected findings
  • Platform consistency. A result for the selected device exposed only another platform's store link. I recommended a compatible destination or a clearly labelled fallback.
  • Filter continuity. Platform and multiplayer preferences were not visibly carried into refinement filters. I recommended preserving selections with removable chips.
  • Recommendation context. Results lacked the selected reference games and match explanations. I recommended a short context summary and specific reasons for individual matches.

The review covered signed-out public interactions on desktop.
