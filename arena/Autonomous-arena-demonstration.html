Autonomous Arena

A self-initiated 3v3 decision-making demo. Both teams pursue possession, support a carrier, pass under pressure and attempt shots. This is a generic experiment, not an implementation of Tilt or prior client work.

60-second match · fixed simulation timestep · seed controls attributes and stochastic decisions · no server, tracking, assets or paid dependencies. Deliberately basic 2D movement and disc physics; no animation, obstacles, contact physics or production balance.
